Planner vs solver on generated Kleine Binckhorst scenarios

20 scenarios per cell, 5 train counts × 3 matchings = 300 scenarios; planner (symbolic-rail, image edge) and solver run on identical instances; generator, solver and evaluator stable/stable/stable; no servicing; 300 s per scenario for both tools. A scenario counts as solved only if the TORS evaluator accepts the plan.

1. How many scenarios are solved?

Overall the two tools are level (80% planner, 79% solver). The planner is clearly ahead on random matching; the solver is ahead on FIFO and LIFO.

Failures start at 2–3 trains and are dominated by late departures and search timeouts.
